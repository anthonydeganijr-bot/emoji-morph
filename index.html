How to Play

Each row of emoji is a picture clue for a word or phrase. Figure it out and type your guess.

  • One new rung unlocks every day — solve it to keep climbing.
  • Rungs get harder as you climb: simple words near the bottom, tricky idioms near the top.
  • A wrong guess just slips you back a step — no penalty, try again.
  • Stuck? Tap Parent hint for a nudge toward the theme.

This is your own personal staircase — nobody else sees the same pace as you.
